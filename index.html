Incrementality

The $2.7M–$5.9M deep-linking study

Measured the true lift of iOS deep-linking in paid search for a national retailer — a quasi-experimental analysis in GA4 and BigQuery separating real incremental revenue from organic cannibalization.

Directly reshaped the client's channel budget
Statistical Analysis

Proving a weather-driven sales drop

A holiday weekend's sales dropped, and weather was the suspected cause. I grouped 51-state data into wet and dry cohorts and re-tested at finer cuts: wet states fell ~12% while dry states rose ~26%, significant at >99% confidence.

Presented to the marketing executive team
Data Quality

Filtering 767K bot sessions

Identified bot traffic in a luxury brand's Direct channel using GA4 engagement and geo signatures — 20% of US traffic — then built the exclusion filter that restored the true channel mix.

True conversion rate restored: 0.7% → 0.9%
Attribution Forensics

When attribution quietly breaks

A B2B marketplace's referral pages were collapsing into the homepage. Root cause wasn't GA4 settings — it was browser Referrer-Policy. Confirmed with client-ID matching, session sequencing, and control tests.

Trustworthy channel reporting restored
Brand Measurement

Finding TV's impact inside Direct traffic

TV spend rose 21% while TV-tagged visits fell to near zero — and Direct traffic grew 52% to 1.36M. I showed a cruise line where its brand advertising was actually landing.

Reshaped how stakeholders credit brand vs. performance
